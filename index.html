Hello! I'm a Master's student in Computer Engineering and Global Design, currently a predoctoral visiting researcher at the TIDE Lab (Universitat Pompeu Fabra), where I'm looking into the societal impacts of genAI in education — specifically the impacts of chatbot roles on output overreliance in a computer-supported collaborative learning environment.

The rest of my work tends to circle the same question from different angles: how do technologies that claim to serve people actually affect them? I've looked at this through animal welfare (Sentient Futures fellowship), democratic participation (Sunflower, a civic tech platform I co-founded), climate information access (Climate Cardinals, where I was Deputy Director of Education), and AI governance more broadly (Chatham House CFC, AFFINE, AISPP).

I work in French and English, occasionally in Spanish, and have the right to work in the EU and UK.
